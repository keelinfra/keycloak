# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

This repository is no longer maintained and reports will not be fixed.
Vulnerabilities in Keycloak itself go to the
[Keycloak project](https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak/security/policy).

## Supported versions

**None.** The distribution was discontinued on 24 October 2026. The earlier
commitment to support it until at least 31 December 2031 is withdrawn: no
release of the distribution or of the keelinfra LTS builds of Keycloak
(`kc-<tag>-keel<n>`) receives security updates. No subscription or support
contract was ever sold for it.

Move to a maintained Keycloak: the upstream [Keycloak](https://www.keycloak.org/)
project, or a commercially supported build such as the Red Hat build of Keycloak.
